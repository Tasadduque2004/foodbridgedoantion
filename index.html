Questions people ask first

Quick answers before you sign up.

Is FoodBridge free?

Yes. Donors and NGOs can join and use FoodBridge at no cost.

When does my phone number or address become visible?

Your phone, email and exact address stay private until an NGO accepts your donation.

What can I donate?

Fresh, safe, unspoiled food such as cooked meals, bakery items and packaged food. Add the quantity and pickup time so NGOs can plan.

How does an NGO collect the food?

The NGO accepts the donation, schedules a pickup time, and chats with the donor to confirm details.
